How to play?

  • Step 1: Create Project
    • Fill in the Project name by naming your project.
    • Click on green button - Create Project.
    • You will now be able to see your new project under > Your Project.
    • Click on Select to access the project.
    • You will then be directed to a page where you will have the access to - Project Setup & Cycle Dashboard.
  • Step 2: Fill in Project - Start Overview: Critical Success Factors (CSF) and Key Performance Indicators (KPI)
    • Under Project Setup > Select Project Start.
    • The initial parameters are determined through extensive research using Google, or you can direct your inquiries to ChatGPT for the formulation of these initial parameters. Specify the Critical Success Factors and Key Performance Indicators, then Submit your results.
    • Your functional areas like Marketing, Finance, Innovation, Operations, Organization Management will be all based on the assumptions that you make.
  • Step 3: Submit Project - Start

    Click on Submit.

  • Step 4: Generating Cycles
    • Select Cycle Dashboard under - Your Project > Select the project > Cycles > Cycle Dashboard.
    • Name your Cycle.
    • Select the Underlying Cycle. When you are generating first cycle you will not be required to select anything the automatic selection of Select if applicable will be applied.
    • Click on Green Button > Create Cycle.
    • Under Select Current Cycle > You will see your cycle.
    • Click on Select to access your Cycle. You will be directed to Input & Output Functional Areas.

    For more information on cycles please refer Help.

  • Step 5: Edit Marketing Cycle
    • In order to edit the Marketing cycle simply go on Input Parameters and select Marketing Management
  • Step 6: Input Advertising Costs
    • Select Add Advertising Cost. You will have a form opened up for adding Advertising Cost. Advertising costs include expenses for advertising, for example, trade shows, promotions, and digital marketing.
    • Name your Advertising Cost, add the advertising cost for FY1. Under FY2 Multiplier, add how much you are planning to increase your advertising cost in 2nd year and 3rd year in FY3 Multiplier. The cost will be automatically calculated in FY2 Value & FY3 Value.
  • Step 7: Input Product Name & Description
    • Select Add Product and you will be directed to a form where you will have various elements to fill in.
    • You can find more details about Product ID, Product Name, Product Distribution, Material Cost, Labor Cost, Other Cost, Price, Units, Cutoff under Help section.

    You must have a minimum of 8 products, with at least 6 designated for retail, and the remaining products intended for wholesale. The wholesale products are essentially the retail items offered in bulk and will be labeled accordingly.

    The special product, which is not part of the initial 8 listed products, will be considered as the 9th product.

  • Step 8: Input Targeted Market Size (per product and FY)

    Allocate the percentage of annual demand for each product for both retail and wholesale, ensuring that the total sums up to 100%.

  • Step 9: Input Sales Projection

    The monthly demand for each product fluctuates based on seasonality and the specific needs associated with each product.

    Ensure that the sum of the demands adds up to 100%. Any deviation from this total will be highlighted in red.

  • Step 10: Submission of Marketing cycle

    After inputting all the parameters in the Marketing tab, click "Submit." Your cycle will be saved.

  • Step 11: Move on to Financial Cycle
    • Select Cycle Dashboard under - Your Project > Select the project > Cycles > Cycle Dashboard.
    • Name your Cycle.
    • Select the Underlying Cycle. Select your previous cycle
    • Click on Green Button > Create Cycle.
    • Under Select Current Cycle > You will see your cycle.
    • Click on Select to access your Cycle. You will be directed to Input & Output Functional Areas.
    • Under Input Parameters select Financial Management
  • Step 12: Edit Financial Management

    Conduct your research and input all the parameters mentioned in the Financial Management Tab.

    Financial Management deals primarily with the fixed costs associated with running your business. Here you may edit assumptions about salary and salary growth for various types of employees, as well as how many staff members you need. You may also change the cost of utilities, how much you wish to spend on advertising, the cost of rent, and the amount and nature of initial loans.

    For more information on other financial parameters please refer Help.

  • Step 13: Evaluate Output Parameter - 36 Month Financial Progress

    Under Output parameters, select the 36 Month Financial Progress tab.

    Here you can compare the effect of Marketing cycle vs Financial cycle on the the financial summary. sales projections, Prices and Variable costs per product, Annual Fixed Costs, and 12 month performance of your BrewPub.
